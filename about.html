About Kitchen Constants

Created by Amos Chiam

Good ingredients deserve better than guesswork.

I built Kitchen Constants to collect the cooking references I kept coming back to, from dry-brining salt percentages and meat temperatures to pasta dough and baking ratios. Instead of searching through recipes, videos, and forum posts every time I cooked, I wanted everything in one place with clear, practical guidance.

If you’re the kind of home cook who keeps looking up the same cooking references, I hope you’ll find it useful too.
