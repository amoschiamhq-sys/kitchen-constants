A little context

Why Kitchen Constants exists

Kitchen Constants is a practical reference for home cooks who want good results with minimal effort and less guesswork.

Created by Amos Chiam

I built it because I enjoy cooking at home, but I was tired of wasting good meat and time by using too much salt, too little salt, or cooking it longer than I should have.

The goal is simple: waste less, guess less and make better food.
